About the data

Nearly all published polling on this topic is from the United States. Each survey uses its own wording and sample, so the figures show a pattern and are not comparable point for point.

Pew, Gallup, and AP-NORC are nonpartisan pollsters. Several polls about conditions were commissioned by groups with a position, including Rewiring America, Third Way, the Rainey Center, and America Is All In. EPRI is funded by the power sector.

Every figure links to where it was reported. Where the original survey was not directly available, the link goes to the coverage that quoted it. Compiled 18 November 2026.
